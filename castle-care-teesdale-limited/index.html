What we do

What home care looks like with us

Care at home means the small, ordinary things done well: a wash, a meal, a chat, a lift to the day centre. Here is how we shape it around you.

01 Personal care

Help with the personal things, done with dignity.

Washing, dressing, getting up in the morning and settling at night. Meals, medication prompts, and a hand with the bits that have become harder. Always at your pace, and always with respect.

02 Companionship and daily living

Company, and a hand around the house.

A chat over tea, a walk to the shops, help with cooking, laundry and keeping on top of the home. The everyday things that make a day feel like yours.

03 Specialist support

Care shaped around a condition.

Experienced with support for sensory impairments, dementia care, support for physical disabilities. Care plans are written with the person and their family, and reviewed as things change. Every plan is written with you and reviewed as needs change.

04 Respite for family carers

A break for the people who do the caring.

Looking after someone you love is a big job. We can step in for a few hours, a day or longer, so you can rest knowing they are in good hands.

How care starts

Three steps, at your speed

Nothing happens until you are ready. Most families start with a phone call and a cup of tea.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    Call us, or ask us to call you. Tell us what is happening and what would help. It is fine if you do not have all the answers yet.

  2. 02

    A home visit

    We come to see you at home, meet the family if you would like, and listen to what matters. Together we write a care plan around it.

  3. 03

    Care begins

    You meet your carers, visits start on the days and times that suit you, and we keep checking that the plan still fits.
